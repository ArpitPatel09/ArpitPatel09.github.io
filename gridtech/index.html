    GridTech Hackathon · interactive model

    A data center is ready before the grid is.

    A 75 MW AI campus can open in month 14, but the utility can only deliver 25 MW at month 18, 50 MW at month 24 and the full 75 MW at month 30. Until then it must bridge the gap with solar and wind contracts, onsite gas, fuel cells, batteries or a future nuclear deal, all squeezed through one shared grid wire and an 8-acre pad.

    1. InputsSite, demand ramp, grid schedule, prices, ERCOT hourly shapes
    2. 87,600-hour dispatchEvery hour for ten years: merit order, battery, stress windows, last-resort relocation
    3. Portfolio searchLocal search over a carbon-price ladder builds a cost-vs-CO₂ Pareto library
    4. RankingHard limits filter, then augmented Tchebycheff on speed, cost and climate
    5. RecommendationShown here in 3D, hour by hour, with its cost and emissions

    The engine in this page is a JavaScript port of the Python/numba hackathon model. Its default plan reproduces the checker-verified submission to within a dollar: $354.9M over ten years, $70.20/MWh, 53.5 kg CO₂/MWh.
