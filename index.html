Estimating the SOC you can't measure

Benchmarked Coulomb counting against a Kalman filter on a pack model with sensor noise and a wrong initial SOC. Coulomb counting carries any initial error forever and drifts with a biased sensor; the Kalman filter recovers. Tuning Q, R and P₀ showed it's their ratio, not their size, that sets how hard the filter corrects.

Designed to stay inside the thermal window

Every charging model in my e-scooter pack build carried a thermal path — temperature was a design input, not an afterthought. Across all simulation runs the cell peaked well inside the typical 0–45 °C Li-ion charging window. The trade-off is explicit: halving the C-rate cut the peak from 31 °C to 23.5 °C for one extra hour of charging.

  • Voltage ceilingCV phase holds 4.2 V per cell; PI controller output saturated between 3.7 and 4.5 V.
  • Charge terminationCurrent tapers in CV and charging stops at C/30, before any overcharge.
  • Thermal modelLumped thermal mass (44.1 J/K), convection to ambient, and a 25 K/W cell-to-ambient path in the pack.
  • InterlocksStateflow blocks charging when full and shuts the load off at minimum voltage.
  • Balancing heatBleed current limited to about 0.3 A so resistors don't heat neighbouring cells.

Electrochemical Intercalation & Form Factors

Unlike lead-acid or NiMH batteries that undergo destructive chemical phase changes, lithium-ion cells store energy through reversible intercalation: Li⁺ ions insert into the host graphite interstitial lattice during charge and extract into metal-oxide cathodes during discharge. Explored the electrochemistry, safety profiles, and mechanical packaging of cylindrical jelly-rolls, prismatic hard cans, and ultra-thin pouch cells.

Strict 4-Level Priority System Hierarchy

A Battery Management System (BMS) is an automotive-grade embedded system governing multi-cell packs under a non-negotiable four-level priority hierarchy: Safety always preempts Protection, which overrides Performance Management, which supersedes Host Interfaces. Designed the functional safety logic to protect human operators, prevent cell abuse, and compute safe operational boundaries.

Priority 1 · SafetyPyrofuse & high-voltage contactor disconnect, thermal runaway venting detection

Priority 3 · PerformanceEKF SOC/SOH estimation, continuous & pulse power limits, passive cell balancing
Priority 4 · InterfaceAutomotive CAN 2.0B / CAN-FD, precharge resistor timing, and black-box telemetry

4-Wire Kelvin Sensing & AFE Instrumentation

During high-current acceleration or regen pulses (up to 100 A+), cable resistance causes massive IR voltage drops (50–200 mV) that corrupt state estimation. Solved this using a 4-Wire Kelvin Sensing Bridge: high-current power cables are physically separated from high-impedance sense lines (I_sense ≈ 0), delivering pure electrochemical potential directly to 16-bit differential Analog Front-End (AFE) ADCs. Combined with Manganin shunts and Hall-effect sensors for current measurement.

Extended Kalman Filtering vs. Coulomb Counting

Coulomb counting integrates current over time but inevitably drifts due to sensor bias and unknown initial state. Developed an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) that pairs Coulomb counting with the ESC cell voltage model: measurement innovation vectors continuously correct state estimates, bounding SOC error within ±1.0% across dynamic EV drive profiles while enforcing Safe Operating Area (SOA) voltage and temperature boundaries.

Enhanced Self-Correcting (ESC) State-Space Model

Built a phenomenological equivalent circuit model capturing all dominant lithium-ion cell dynamics: thermodynamic chemical potential OCV(z, T), instantaneous ohmic resistance R₀, two parallel RC branches (R₁||C₁ for fast charge-transfer and R₂||C₂ for slow solid-state diffusion), and dynamic hysteresis h(t) encoding charge-direction memory. Formulated into discrete-time state-space difference equations (x[k+1] = A_d·x[k] + B_d·u[k]) optimized for real-time microcontrollers.

4 State VariablesState vector: SOC z, fast diffusion i_R1, slow diffusion i_R2, and hysteresis h
Instantaneous R₀Ohmic resistance producing the instantaneous voltage step (ΔV = I·R₀)
2 RC Diffusion PairsFast kinetics (τ₁ ≈ 10s) and slow solid-state mass diffusion (τ₂ ≈ 150s)
Hysteresis h[k]First-order differential state driven by signed current direction sign(i[k])
