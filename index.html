Battery Controls03 / 06

Closed-Loop Li-ion Charger Control

Built and tuned PI controllers in Simulink and Simscape that charge a Panasonic 18650 cell in constant-current, constant-voltage and constant-power modes, with temperature and SOC tracked through a lumped thermal model. Then took it down to the power stage: a PWM buck converter whose duty cycle is set by a PI loop on measured current. Key finding: matching the proportional gain to the cell's total internal impedance removed current spikes.

04 — Battery Engineering

Cell to Pack to BMS

Two battery courses worked end to end in MATLAB, Simulink and Simscape Battery, from electrochemistry up to supervisory BMS logic. Thermal limits were a design input from the start.

E6590 Battery Systems

From half-reactions to terminal voltage

Derived cell voltage and theoretical specific energy from half-reactions, then modeled the Nernst shift with temperature and the three polarization losses that pull terminal voltage below open-circuit voltage. Every curve was scripted in MATLAB.

  • 792 Wh/kgNa–S theoretical specific energy
  • 3 lossesOhmic, activation, concentration
  • −25 to 50 °CTemperature sweeps of the Nernst voltage
Both courses

Equivalent-circuit models, fitted to data

Built Thevenin models in Simulink with OCV and R₀ lookup tables, compared 0, 1 and 3 RC pairs on a pulse profile to see what the extra dynamics buy, and used least squares to identify OCV curves and internal resistance from measurements at four temperatures. Projected capacity fade to end of life.

  • 0 · 1 · 3RC-pair fidelity compared on pulse loads
  • 4 tempsLeast-squares Rint from −25 to 50 °C
  • ~1,200Cycles to 80% EOL from measured fade

Battery Design & Management

Estimating the SOC you can't measure

Benchmarked Coulomb counting against a Kalman filter on a pack model with sensor noise and a wrong initial SOC. Coulomb counting carries any initial error forever and drifts with a biased sensor; the Kalman filter recovers. Tuning Q, R and P₀ showed it's their ratio, not their size, that sets how hard the filter corrects.

  • 3 sensorsCurrent, voltage, temperature with noise
  • Q · R · P₀Covariances tuned for convergence vs. overshoot
  • 1 RC pairPack model fidelity used by the estimator
Battery Design & Management

Passive balancing and a Stateflow BMS

Added SOC-based passive balancing to the series string, driven by the Kalman estimate rather than the model's hidden SOC, and tightened the threshold until module mismatch stopped eating capacity. Closed the loop with a Stateflow chart that sequences discharge, CC and CV, then turned it into an automated charge–discharge cycle tester.

  • 0.005SOC balancing threshold, down from 0.2
  • ~0.3 ABleed current, about 15 Ω, kept cool
  • 0.3 ↔ 0.9SOC band for automated cycle testing
Thermal Safety

Designed to stay inside the thermal window

Every charging model carried a thermal path, so temperature was something I could see and design against. Across all my runs the cell peaked well inside the typical 0–45 °C Li-ion charging window. The trade-off is explicit: halving the C-rate cut the peak from 31 °C to 23.5 °C for one extra hour of charging.

  • Voltage ceilingCV phase holds 4.2 V per cell; PI controller output saturated between 3.7 and 4.5 V.
  • Charge terminationCurrent tapers in CV and charging stops at C/30, before any overcharge.
  • Thermal modelLumped thermal mass (44.1 J/K), convection to ambient, and a 25 K/W cell-to-ambient path in the pack.
  • InterlocksStateflow blocks charging when full and shuts the load off at minimum voltage.
  • Balancing heatBleed current limited to about 0.3 A so resistors don't heat neighbouring cells.
