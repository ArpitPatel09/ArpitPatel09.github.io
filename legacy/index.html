About Me

I’ve always learned best by taking things apart. Long before I started modeling grid decarbonization or running battery optimization algorithms, I was the kid replacing an iPad screen on the kitchen table or designing a 3D-printed, obstacle-avoiding robot from the ground up.

Today, I’m an Earth and Environmental Engineering Master’s student at Columbia University, focused on energy systems economics. With a Mechanical Engineering background from Shiv Nadar University, I bring that same hands-on, build-it-to-break-it energy into my technical work.

Whether I’m running techno-economic analyses for carbon capture, dispatching battery storage models, or predicting peak demand on the NYCA grid, I don’t just want the numbers to balance—I want to understand exactly how the system works in the real world.
